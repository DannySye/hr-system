# Phase 10: Disciplinary & Statutory Fair Process

When an employee breaches workplace rules, procedural fairness is as vital as the rule itself. An unfair or undocumented process exposes the organization to legal liability under the ACAS Code of Practice.

## The 6 Mandatory Steps of Statutory Fair Process
1. **Identify the specific issue**
2. **Establish the objective facts**
3. **Provide full opportunity for the employee to respond**
4. **Follow standard company procedure (advance notice, accompaniment)**
5. **Make a proportionate, evidence-backed decision**
6. **Formally document outcome with right of appeal**
