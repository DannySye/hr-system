# Phase 1: Workforce Planning & Job Analysis

Before advertising any role, HR must answer a fundamental strategic question: does the organization truly need this headcount, and what does success in this role look like?

## Key Principles of Workforce Planning
- **Job Description (JD) vs. Person Specification (PS)**: The JD describes the *job* (duties, working conditions, KPIs). The PS describes the *person* (qualifications, experience, essential vs. desirable criteria).
- **New vs. Replacement**: Replacement roles inherit existing benchmarks; new roles require full business case justification.
- **Equality Act 2010 Compliance**: Essential criteria must be bona fide occupational requirements (e.g. valid driving license for field deployments) rather than arbitrary filters.
