# Phase 6: Probation Management & Continuous Operations

A new hire isn't "done" the moment they sign an employment contract — probation is the structured operational window where both employer and employee confirm mutual competency and cultural alignment.

<TryItNow label="Open the probation objective setter" href="#probation-objectives" />

## Key Principles of Effective Probation
- **Probation is proactive, not passive**: Establish clear, observable benchmarks on Day 1.
- **Continuous attendance & leave monitoring**: Patterns of late arrival or unnotified absences accumulate over time; monitoring registers daily surfaces early signals before they escalate.
