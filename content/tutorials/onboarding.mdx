# Phase 5: Onboarding Architecture & First-Month Induction

Onboarding transforms a signed contract into a productive, culturally integrated employee through structured orientation and supervisory clarity.

## The 3 Pillars of Structured Onboarding
1. **About the Company**: Mission, culture, organizational structure, executive introductions.
2. **About the Job**: Role responsibilities, milestones, hardware tool provisioning, pair buddy.
3. **Workplace Rules**: Working hours, on-call escalation, leave requests, UK GDPR, and safety SOPs.
