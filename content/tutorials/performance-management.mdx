# Phase 7: Performance Management & 360° Feedback

Performance management done right isn't an annual shock — it is measurable KPIs established early, monitored continuously, and appraised through multi-perspective 360-degree feedback.

<TryItNow label="Open the KPI builder" href="#kpi-builder" />

## The Power of 360-Degree Feedback
- **Triangulated Perspective**: Self-assessments capture employee motivations; managerial reviews evaluate output; peer colleague feedback reveals collaboration and daily friction.
- **Actionable Outcomes**: An effective appraisal synthesizes findings into concrete strengths, addressable weaknesses, and specific training needs.
