# Phase 9: Employee Welfare & Grievance Procedures

HR is often the primary confidential channel an employee has to voice concerns before burnout or workplace conflict damages organizational stability.

## Welfare vs. Formal Grievances
- **Proactive Welfare**: Routine check-ins on workload sustainability, ergonomics, and mental wellbeing.
- **Formal Grievance**: A formal complaint raised by an employee regarding unfair treatment, contract breach, or unaddressed workload distress.
