# Phase 4: Offer Letters, Reference Checks & Employment Contracts

Selecting the preferred candidate must be followed by independent verification and formal written employment terms governed by the UK Employment Rights Act 1996.

## Statutory Contract Requirements
- **Core Terms**: Job title, salary, working hours, workplace location, holiday entitlement, probation period, and notice period.
- **Independent Verification**: Professional references confirm employment dates and verify conduct before contract execution.
