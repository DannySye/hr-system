# Phase 8: Learning & Development Needs Analysis

Identifying a development gap during an appraisal and doing nothing about it wastes the diagnostic effort. This stage bridges performance diagnosis and actionable catalog fulfillment.

## Four Core Training Categories
1. **Induction**: Organizational values, workplace setup, compliance orientation.
2. **Technical**: Role-specific engineering, software tools, data pipelines.
3. **Soft Skills**: Conflict de-escalation, structured communication, teamwork.
4. **Compliance**: UK GDPR, health & safety, anti-harassment regulations.
