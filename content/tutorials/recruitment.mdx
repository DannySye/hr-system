# Phase 2: Sourcing Strategy & Job Adverts

Recruitment is about reaching the right pool of qualified talent through targeted sourcing channels with an advert that accurately communicates expectations.

## Strategic Channel Selection
- **Channel Fit**: Field telecom engineering requires technical job boards and technical college partnerships, not generic social blasts.
- **Accurate Ad Copy**: Clear salary bands, working hours, on-call expectations, and definitive closing dates reduce drop-offs.
